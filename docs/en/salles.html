Easiest · recommended

1. Give us your calendar link

If your events are already in an online calendar, give us its address once. It will be read automatically every day: additions, changes and cancellations show up here with nothing more to do.

  • Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → check “Make available to public”, then copy the “Public address in iCal format”.
  • Other tools (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix sites, ticketing platforms…): look for an “iCal”, “.ics”, “Subscribe” or “Export calendar” link.
  • No such link? Just give us the address of your “Programming” or “Events” page: we'll read it automatically.

3. Write to us

A question, a correction or a removal:

What we publish

The title, date, time, venue and a link to your page. The calendar doesn't sell tickets and doesn't copy your texts or images. A venue's first addition is checked by a person before it is published.

A mistake, or want to be removed?

Write to us and we'll fix it quickly. Events also come from public listings (City of Montréal, cultural calendars): if something is wrong, the original source is shown on each event.

Your information

The email address asked for in the forms is only used to reach you about your events. It is never published or shared.
